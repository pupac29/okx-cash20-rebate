### Maker versus taker fees

A **maker** order adds liquidity to the order book and is not filled immediately. A **taker** order matches an existing order and is filled immediately. Market orders are generally treated as taker orders because they execute against available liquidity.

For someone using OKX casually, the taker fee is often the more relevant number because market orders and immediately executed limit orders can incur the taker rate. OKX explains that the exact fee is calculated according to the filled amount and the fee rate for the product and account tier.

A referral rebate may lower eligible trading costs, but it does not change the fact that frequent trading creates more fee events. A lower percentage applied repeatedly can still become expensive if the strategy involves constant buying and selling.
